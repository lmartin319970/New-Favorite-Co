Miso is a fermented paste commonly made from soybeans, salt, and koji. It can add complexity to marinades, dressings, soups, and glazes.

## Where to start

Try whisking a small amount of white miso into softened butter, then using it on vegetables or fish. Start modestly: miso is already salty.
