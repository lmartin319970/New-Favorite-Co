## Roasted Chicken

### Ingredients

#### Chicken & Wet Brine

- 4 **Whole Chicken Quarters**

- ¼ cup **white vinegar**

- 60 g **salt**, or ¼ cup if using Morton kosher salt.

- 8 cups **Cold water**

#### Seasoning Paste

- 2 tsp **Paprika**

- 1 ½ tsp **Cumin**

- ¾ tsp **Cinnamon**

- 2 tsp **Onion Powder**

- 2 ½ tsp **Garlic Powder**

- 1 tsp **dried Thyme**

- ½ tbsp **Olive Oil**

- ½ tbsp **Orange Juice**

#### Honey Butter Glaze

- 3 tbsp **Honey**

- 3 tbsp **Butter**

### Instructions

#### Chicken Brine

1. Add chicken, vinegar, salt, and water to bowl. Cover and place in fridge for 4-8 hours.

#### Roasted Chicken

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.

1. Combine all seasoning ingredients listed above into a paste, adding more spices or olive oil as necessary.

1. Take chicken out of the brine and pat dry.

1. Rub seasoning paste on chicken, fully coating it, including under the skin when feasible.

1. Place on greased wire rack and baking pan; bake at 425 for 40-45 minutes.

1. Combine melted butter, honey, and a pinch of salt. Glaze chicken with mixture, and bake for 5 more minutes.

1. Ensure chicken reaches 165°F before removing.

1. Rest for 10 minutes.

## Cornbread Stuffing

### Ingredients

#### Buttermilk Cornbread

- 1 cup **yellow cornmeal**

- 1 cup **all-purpose flour**

- ¼ cup **granulated sugar**

- 1 tbsp **baking powder**

- ½ tsp **baking soda**

- Big pinch of **salt**

- 1 cup **buttermilk**

- 2 **large eggs**

- ½ cup **unsalted butter**

#### Stuffing

- ¾ lb **breakfast sausage**

- 1 **large onion**

- 2 sticks **celery**

- 2 tbsp **fresh Sage leaves**

- 1 tbsp **fresh Thyme**

- 1 tsp **fresh Rosemary**

- 5 cloves **Garlic**

- 1 ½ - 2 cups **Low sodium chicken broth**

### Instructions

#### Buttermilk Cornbread

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.

1. Combine dry ingredients: flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in bowl. Mix thoroughly.

1. Combine wet ingredients: buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter in a separate bowl. Mix thoroughly.

1. Combine wet and dry ingredients, mix thoroughly and add it to a well-greased (rubbing on butter or non-stick spray) 8-inch oven pan.

1. Bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes. Ensure a center-dipped fork comes out dry. For stuffing, better on the drier side.

1. Cover and refrigerate (if being extra safe) until ready to make stuffing.

#### Cornbread Stuffing

1. Cut cornbread into ½ - ¾ inch cubes. Spread out on greased baking sheet and place in oven with chicken or at 425°F for 15 minutes, or until slightly dry.

1. Not croutons, but with less moisture, so the cornbread crumbles rather than smushes.

1. Cook down breakfast sausage until brown and crumbled. Once ready, remove from pan but keep grease.

1. Keep pan on medium heat. Dice onions and celery and cook down for about 7 minutes.

1. Place garlic, thyme, rosemary and sage and mix in pan until fragrant. Take off heat once ready.

1. Mix veggie mixture with cornbread and sausage in a larger 9×13-inch oven pan. Make sure it’s mixed well, but be careful to not crumble cornbread too much.

1. Add broth to mixture. You don’t really want it pooling at the bottom, but ensure no cornbread is dry. A very thin pool at the bottom that dries shortly is acceptable.

1. Bake at 350 covered in foil for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes.

   - If the oven is still at 425, you can bake for 15 minutes covered, and 10 minutes uncovered instead


1. Rest for 5-10 minutes before serving.

1. Serve and top with fresh thyme if you have it. Enjoy!

## Cranberry Sauce

### Ingredients

- 12 oz **fresh cranberries**

- ¾ cup **white sugar**

- 1/3 cup **brown sugar**

- 2 **Oranges**, juiced and zested

- ½ cup **water**

- ½ tsp **Cinnamon**

- 2 tbsp **Butter**

- 1 tbsp **Vanilla Bean Paste**

- ½ tsp **Salt**, adjust to taste

### Instructions

1. Add water, orange juice, brown sugar and white sugar to medium saucepan. Heat to light simmer.

1. Add cranberries to saucepan for 10-15 minutes, or until most cranberries have burst. As they cook down, use spatula to crush cranberries to desired consistency.

1. Once cooked down and desired consistency is reached, take off heat and add vanilla bean paste, salt, cinnamon and orange zest. Be very careful to not use any pith (white) of the orange.

1. While cooling, place cold butter in sauce and quickly mix it around. This ensures a restaurant-quality gloss.

1. Taste the sauce; if needed, add more sugar. Cranberries tend to be slightly bitter until sufficiently sweetened.

1. Store in fridge until ready to serve.
